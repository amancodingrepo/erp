Production risk checklist

Tenant authorizationNever trust a browser-supplied School ID; enforce user role and school scope on every API request.
Template versioningVersion templates by board, session, class and subject; freeze the formula used for issued cards.
AuditabilityRecord old/new marks, user, time, reason, approval and publication/reprint history.
Safe bulk importValidate the whole file before committing; reject duplicates, unknown students, malformed headers and out-of-range values.
Calculation correctnessMake weights, rounding, pass rules, exemptions, absent status and practical/internal components explicit.
Publication guardShow missing assessments, prevent silent overwrites and preserve published snapshots.

Scope limitation

Browser prototype only: no real login, server-side authorization, API, database, schema change or claim of a complete official report-card proforma. Four templates and fictional learners demonstrate workflow only.
